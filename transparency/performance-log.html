Transparency

Performance Log

Last updated 16 October 2026

Every material error found in our work, how it was found and what changed as a result. Errors caught before publication sit alongside those that reached a published product, because our checks are only worth something if we can show what they catch.

How the Log Works

  • An error is logged if it would change a figure, a finding or what a reader takes from a product. Typing and style fixes are not logged
  • Each entry records where the error got to, what kind of error it was, which part of our process produced it, how it was found and what we changed
  • An error that reached a published product is also corrected in that product or in the next edition, and the correction says so on its face
  • Entries are never rewritten after the event. The only later change is closing an open entry once its fix is made and checked
  • Two errors of the same kind from the same part of the process within 90 days put that part on probation. The site owner then reviews every output from it line by line until it has produced three products in a row with nothing logged
