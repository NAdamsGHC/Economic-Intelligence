Horizon Calendar

The dates coming up that matter for Gateshead's economy. Data releases, consultations closing, funding windows, fiscal events and policy changes taking effect, in one place. Every date is checked at the source named against it, and the calendar is refreshed with each weekly What's New and each monthly update.

Reading the priorities. Red means we need to act by or on the day. Amber means the date feeds one of our products or needs preparation. Green means noted, with no action expected.

Reading the status. Confirmed means the publisher has fixed the day. Provisional means the publisher has given a date that may still move. Expected means only a month or a season has been published, so the item sits in that month without a day. Release times are UK time.

Coverage. ONS releases are taken from the ONS release calendar to , filtered to the series we use. Business survey dates come from the S&P Global calendar, which runs about a month ahead. Policy dates are taken from the government page linked on each item, never from press coverage.
